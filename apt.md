## \#🔎 APT(Advanced Persistent Threats):

##### &#x20; APTs are prolonged, targeted cyberattacks where intruders gain access to a network and remain undetected for an extended period.



#### &#x20;>⚙️ Goal:

##### &#x20;      Steal sensitive information (intellectual property, financial data, government secrets) rather than cause immediate      

##### &#x20;      disruption.



#### &#x20;> ⚙️Persistence: 

##### &#x20;            Attackers maintain long-term access, often months or years, by using stealthy techniques.



#### &#x20;>🛠 How APT Attacks Work:

##### &#x20;  **1. Reconnaissance:**

###### &#x20;       Attackers study the target’s systems, employees, and vulnerabilities.

###### 

##### &#x20;  **2. Initial Intrusion**:

###### &#x20;     Often via spear-phishing emails, malicious attachments, or exploiting software flaws.

#### 

##### &#x20;  **3. Establish Foothold:** 

###### &#x20;      Install backdoors or malware to maintain access.

#### 

##### &#x20;  **4. Privilege Escalation:** 

###### &#x20;      Gain higher-level permissions to move deeper into the network.

###### 

##### &#x20;  **5. Lateral Movement:**

###### &#x20;     Spread across systems to access valuable data.

#### 

##### &#x20;  **6. Data Exfiltration:** 

###### &#x20;     Steal sensitive information quietly over time.

###### 

##### &#x20;  **7. Persistence:**

###### &#x20;     Use hidden tools to remain undetected even after partial cleanups.

###### 



#### 🔒Preventions:

###### &#x20;1.Threat intelligence monitoring to detect unusual activity.

###### 

###### &#x20;2.Zero Trust architecture to limit lateral movement.

###### 

###### &#x20;3.Regular patching to close vulnerabilities.

###### 

###### &#x20;4.Employee training against spear-phishing.

###### 

###### &#x20;5.Advanced detection tools like EDR (Endpoint Detection \& Response) and SIEM (Security Information and Event Management).

## \#🛡️ Anonymous/ Ghost hacker:

##### &#x20;Anonymous and GhostSec are two well-known hacktivist groups. Anonymous is a decentralized global collective famous for cyberattacks against governments and corporations, while GhostSec originated within Anonymous but later shifted toward both ideological and financially motivated hacking.



#### 1\.🎭 Anonymous (Hacktivist Collective):

##### &#x20; >🔎 Founded:

###### &#x20;           Around 2003 on the imageboard 4chan.

##### 

##### &#x20; >📌 Structure: 

###### &#x20;             Decentralized, leaderless, operating as a “hive mind.”

##### 

##### &#x20; >🎭 Symbol:

###### &#x20;         Guy Fawkes mask (popularized by V for Vendetta).

##### 

##### &#x20; >📖 Philosophy:

###### &#x20;            Anti-censorship, anti-surveillance, and resistance against government or corporate oppression.

###### &#x20;

##### &#x20; >🎯 Tactics:

###### &#x20;           a) Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks

###### &#x20;           b) Website defacement

###### &#x20;           c) Data leaks

##### 

##### &#x20; >🛠 Notable Operations:

###### &#x20;                    a) Attacks on the Church of Scientology (Project Chanology)

###### &#x20;                    b) Campaigns against PayPal, Visa, and Mastercard (after they blocked WikiLeaks donations)

###### &#x20;                    c) Support for social movements like Occupy Wall Street and George Floyd protests

##### 

##### &#x20; >🌍 Public Perception:

###### &#x20;                      a)👍 Supporters call them “digital Robin Hoods.”

###### &#x20;                      b)👎 Critics label them “cyber terrorists” or “cyber lynch mobs.
